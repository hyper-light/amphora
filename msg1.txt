oracles: the features they never made are generated, and a REAL's digits are no finding

The generator (synth.rs) makes compounds, WITH and WITH RECURSIVE, derived tables, window functions,
USING and NATURAL joins, row values, UPSERT, views, triggers, STRICT and WITHOUT ROWID tables, each so
that its answer is certain, and a cut that would leave it uncertain is no case (Sel::certain). The
reducer cuts arms, CTEs, sources and window calls. The summary counts checks by feature, is written as
the run goes, and keeps one example of each refusal. An error met before is not cut down again, and a
panic in a setup is named by its text. A query past 4,000,000 of SQLite's instructions is left.

The differential check compares texts with each REAL in floatingpoint.html 1.3.1's digits on both sides
(digits.rs): SQLite 3.54.0's 17 digits of a double whose 15 convert back are the same text, alone, inside
another text or against other digits; any other difference stays one. A value a REAL's text is inside (a
length, hex, JSONB) may be what SQLite gives with its texts at 15 digits.

Amphora is opened with WITHOUT ROWID tables on, as production opens it (crates/amphora/src/db.rs).

Tests (Linux, adacachy2, the oracles' module on the engine): 11 passed, 0 failed, among them that the
generator makes each feature and that each other digits difference stays one. Bench clippy 0. G2 step 3;
AC-T1.1.1, AC-T1.8.6; docs/research/oracles.md.
