# The follower-write candidates on the deterministic model (2026-11-07)

Step 3 of kiln plan 01a1b8d174dc72d18b9d2289b6e8fe11 (goal 9c63f7d4; docs/research/follower-write-starvation.md). Every
candidate on every workload at leader write rates of 10, 100 and 1,000 a second and round trips of 1, 10 and 100 ms,
twenty seeds of 120 simulated seconds each. Simulated time: the numbers are the model's, not a machine's, so CLAUDE.md
§7's quiet-host rule doesn't bear on them; the machine's timings come in steps 4 and 5.

## The model

`crates/amphora/tests/write_starve_model.rs` (`the_ab_sweep`, ignored; run in release with `AMPHORA_TRACES` and
`AMPHORA_MODEL_OUT`). A leader and a writing follower; another voter's acknowledgement makes a commit (the replicated
class: a commit is the append plus a round trip). One way across the network is half the round trip, up to 20% more,
seeded.

- **The leader's own writes** arrive at the rate, Poisson, into a queue of 4,096 (past it an arrival is refused and
  counted). The owner serializes them: one preparation at a time until it resolves (replication.md, "Resolved
  transactions"), so the leader's capacity is one write per round trip plus its preparation. A refused own write is
  prepared again. Today's leader (A0) prepares at its applied point; every other candidate's at its log tail, as the
  floor's session does (replication.md, "the floor session's rule").
- **The follower** writes, waits 100 ms, writes again; each refused write is evaluated again at once, as kiln's
  `store::write` does, until its 30 s deadline (a miss). Its ReadIndex is a round trip at the leader (Ongaro §6.4), then
  it applies to that index, prepares on its applied point and forwards. Every entry appended moves the point, a
  refused one included (`on_base`).
- **A preparation** costs 100 µs and 5 µs a page read. Model parameters, not measured constants: the sensitivity pass
  runs 20 µs a page with the follower writing once a second.
- **Pages and rows** come from the step-2 traces (`2026-11-07-studio-local2-write-starve-traces`), each transaction the
  one recorded at the leader's position (nine of the leader's to one of the follower's), the follower's chosen when it
  prepares, so a write prepared again meets the pages the leader writes then.

The candidates' rules:

| | rule |
|---|---|
| A0 | today: applies only on the entry before it |
| A1 | applies if no entry since its base wrote a page it read; at most 4,096 entries back (Kung and Robinson's window) |
| A1m | A1, page 1 merged when the entry between changed only its size and checksum, unless both allocated (a page number collision) |
| A1r | A1m with allocations renumbered: two allocating writers never collide |
| B | the follower's command runs at the leader, queued behind the leader's own writes |
| Bp | B, ahead of them |
| C | the follower's write takes the leader's write slot: the leader prepares nothing of its own from the grant to the follower's entry's commit (Kung and Robinson's starving transaction, made the rule) |
| D | A1m, then C after two refusals |
| E | applies if no row it read was written since its base (appends rebased); valid by rows but not by pages, the leader rebases it in its slot, ahead of its own writes |
| EC | E, then C after two refusals |
| F | the follower's session runs at the leader, each statement a round trip, holding the slot |
| G | A0, then C after one refusal |

Tests (not ignored): `today_a_followers_write_starves_under_a_steady_leader` (the control: A0 at 100 a second and a 10 ms
round trip, no follower write gets through in 90 s, every one misses its deadline);
`with_no_leader_writes_every_follower_write_applies_first_time` (non-vacuity); `the_lease_and_the_leader_run_candidates_do_not_starve_on_the_control`.

## What it shows

- **The control reproduces the finding.** Today's rule (A0) misses its deadline in 42 of 63 cells. At 100 leader
  writes a second and a 10 ms round trip it lets 3 follower writes through in twenty 120 s runs and misses 60, on
  every workload, same-table appends included: kiln's runner's starvation.
- **Today's leader caps itself at one write a round trip.** The owner serializes its preparations, so alone the leader
  commits about 897 a second at a 1 ms round trip, 98 at 10 ms and 9.7 at 100 ms. Offered more, its queue fills and it
  refuses. Every rate past that ceiling is a saturated leader in every candidate, not a candidate's cost.
- **Page validation alone (A1, A1m, A1r) does not fix it.** It fixes disjoint tables and random keys, but same-table
  appends hit the last leaf (append at 100/s, 10 ms: p50 209 ms, p99 2.7 s; the kiln mix p50 86 ms, p99 1.8 s), and hot
  rows still starve (12 of 63 cells miss, on hot and big at 100 ms). Page 1's merge matters only to writers growing
  different tables (grow at 100/s, 10 ms: A1 p50 84 ms, A1m 42 ms with p99 85 ms, A1r p99 43 ms).
- **No candidate that gives the follower the leader's slot, or runs its write there, misses anywhere:** Bp, C, D, EC
  and G, 0 of 63 cells each. They differ in latency and in what the leader pays:
  - **Bp** (the follower's command at the head of the leader's queue): the lowest latency, a round trip and a hop
    (21.7 ms p50 at 10 ms, 300 ms at 100 ms), the leader keeping at least 0.75 of its rate alone (big at 100/s,
    100 ms), 0.91 at 10 ms. It needs writes as named commands: kiln's closures registered by name on every node.
    Plain B, queued behind the leader's own writes, misses in 21 cells.
  - **C** (the write slot every time): about two round trips (42.8 ms p50 at 10 ms), but the leader holds its slot for
    each follower write, two round trips and more: at worst it keeps 0.48 of its rate (big at 100/s, 100 ms; 1,217 of
    2,400 s held).
  - **G** (today's rule, the slot after one refusal): no engine change. A refused round first (93 ms p50 at 10 ms),
    the leader keeping at least 0.68.
  - **D** (page validation, the slot after two refusals) and **EC** (row-level rebase, the slot after two refusals):
    worst p99 1.38 s (big, 100 ms), the leader keeping at least 0.76 and 0.77. EC's rebase alone (E) misses in 3 cells
    (big at 100 ms, true row conflicts); D needs the read sets (built, step 2) and page 1's size merged.
  - **F** (the session at the leader): each statement a round trip holding the slot; big transactions take the leader
    to 0.01 of its rate.
- **The ranking holds when preparations are four times slower a page and the follower writes once a second:** the
  same candidates miss, and the slot candidates' cost falls (C, D, EC, G keep 0.96 to 0.97 of the leader's rate at
  10 ms, from 0.78 to 0.80): what the leader pays scales with how often the follower writes.
- **False conflicts.** Page validation's refusals on the append and kiln workloads are all rows that rebase (appends);
  on hot rows about half are false (two different counters on one page: 30,523 of 56,715 at 100/s, 10 ms).
